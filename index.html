Research

Publications and presentations

EFAST 2026 · ORAL PRESENTATION

Socioeconomic and Temporal Drivers of Antibiotic Resistance: A Retrospective Analysis of the COVID-19 Impact

We trained a stacking ensemble on ICU patient records to predict antibiotic resistance, then examined how social determinants of health and the pandemic shifted resistance patterns. A central part of the analysis is concept drift: how a clinical model's performance changes when the patient population shifts, as it did after COVID-19.

With Fariha Rahman (first author), Golam Mostofa Naeem, and Pushpo Gondha Paul
